OutLoudBefore you start

A place to practice speaking, where nobody is listening.

  • You pick a situation. You will be asked four questions.
  • You answer out loud, like a real interview.
  • About four minutes. You can stop any time.
  • At the end: one thing that worked, one thing to try.

Nobody hears you. Your voice is never recorded or saved — only your words are sent to the AI that replies.

BackYour answers

What happens to what you say

  • Your voice is not saved. The recording is used to work out what you said, then discarded.
  • Your words go to an AI service so it can ask the next question and write your feedback. The service may use them to improve its AI.
  • Your practice stays on this phone so you can come back and continue.
  • No other person sees any of it. Not other users, not employers.
